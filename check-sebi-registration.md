# How to check if a broker, adviser or fund is SEBI-registered

Before you hand money or trading access to anyone in the securities market, check that they are registered with the **Securities and Exchange Board of India** in the right category. Unregistered "advisers" on messaging apps are among the most common sources of investment fraud.

## Match the person to the category

SEBI registers intermediaries in **23 categories**. IndiaCos lists **27,532 registrations**. The ones retail investors most often need:

| You are dealing with | Category to check | Registrations |
|---|---|---|
| Someone giving you stock tips for a fee | Investment Adviser or Research Analyst | 1,054 / 2,295 |
| A broker opening your trading account | Stock Broker (equity, F&O, currency, commodity, debt, interest rate) | 2,742 equity |
| Someone managing your portfolio | Portfolio Manager | 537 |
| Your demat account provider | Depository Participant (CDSL or NSDL) | 753 / 354 |
| A private fund | Alternative Investment Fund or Venture Capital Fund | 2,035 / 149 |
| A mutual fund | Mutual Fund | 61 |

Other categories include Merchant Bankers, Registrars and Share Transfer Agents, Bankers to an Issue, Debenture Trustees, Custodians, Credit Rating Agencies, InvITs and REITs. The largest category, **Foreign Portfolio Investors (11,490)**, is rarely relevant to retail investors.

## The key distinction: adviser vs analyst vs broker

- An **Investment Adviser** gives personalised advice for a fee.
- A **Research Analyst** publishes research and recommendations.
- A **broker** executes trades. A broker's registration does not make them a registered adviser.

If someone charges you for tips, they should be registered as an adviser or analyst, not just "working with a broker".

## How to check

1. Ask for the **SEBI registration number** and the exact registered name.
2. Search it on the register and confirm the **category and place** match what you were told.
3. Check that the name on your payment receipt or agreement matches the registered entity.
4. Be wary of anyone who guarantees returns, asks you to pay into a personal account, or refuses to share a registration number.

## A limitation to know

The SEBI list does not carry a CIN or PAN, so IndiaCos cannot tie registrations to company records automatically. If the intermediary is a company, check its company record separately.

Search the [SEBI-registered intermediaries list on IndiaCos](https://indiacos.com/sebi-registered), part of its [regulator lists](https://indiacos.com/regulatory), refreshed weekly. For lenders, see [check NBFC RBI registration](check-nbfc-rbi-registration.html).
