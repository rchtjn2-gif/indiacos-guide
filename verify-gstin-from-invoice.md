# How to verify a GST number from an invoice in 30 seconds

Every B2B invoice in India carries the supplier's GSTIN. Checking it takes seconds and can save you from losing input tax credit (ITC) or paying a business that does not exist.

## Why it matters

- **Input tax credit**: you can generally claim ITC only if the supplier is validly registered and actually reports the invoice. A cancelled or fake GSTIN can mean credit is denied and reversed with interest.
- **Fraud**: fake invoices often use made-up or cancelled GSTINs.
- **Wrong entity**: a GSTIN tells you the legal name, which may differ from the brand on the invoice.

## The 15 characters decoded

| Position | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | State code (for example 07 for Delhi, 27 for Maharashtra) |
| 3-12 | The PAN of the business |
| 13 | Entity number: the count of registrations under the same PAN in that state |
| 14 | Usually "Z" by default |
| 15 | Check digit, calculated from the first 14 characters |

The [GSTIN format explained](https://indiacos.com/gstin-format) page on IndiaCos goes through each part in more detail.

## The 30-second check

1. **Look at the shape**: 15 characters, two digits, then a valid-looking PAN, then two characters and a check digit.
2. **Match the state code** to the supplier's address on the invoice.
3. **Look it up**: enter the GSTIN in [IndiaCos GSTIN search](https://indiacos.com/gstin-search) to see the decoded identity, legal name, status (active, cancelled or suspended) and other GSTINs under the same PAN.
4. **Match the name**: the legal or trade name should match the invoice.

## If a supplier's GSTIN is cancelled

- Pause payment of the GST portion until the supplier clarifies.
- Ask for a revised invoice with a valid GSTIN, or the reason for cancellation.
- Check your GSTR-2B to see whether the invoice appears before claiming ITC.
- Speak to your tax adviser about any credit already claimed.

## A simple vendor-onboarding checklist

- [ ] GSTIN verified and active
- [ ] Legal name matches PAN and bank account
- [ ] Company or LLP status checked (CIN or LLPIN)
- [ ] Court cases naming the business reviewed
- [ ] Address matches the registration
