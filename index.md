# IndiaCos

**Know any Indian business before you deal with it.** Official site: [indiacos.com](https://indiacos.com/)

IndiaCos helps you check an Indian business in the two minutes before you sign, pay, lend or ship. Each company, LLP or GST registration gets one report built from public records: MCA company and LLP masters, GST registrations and court records. A report opens with a verdict and summary, then shows identity (CIN, LLPIN or GSTIN decoded, status, age), court cases naming the business with cheque-bounce, fraud and insolvency flags, and size and links: turnover band, every GSTIN under the same PAN, and other businesses at the same address. You can verify a GSTIN from an invoice, search every GSTIN under a PAN, browse by industry, state, director or office, and track new registrations. Reports are free to read and use for your own checks. Every report is also available as JSON through the API, plus an MCP server for AI assistants.
