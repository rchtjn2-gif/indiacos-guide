# Is your customer or supplier in insolvency?

When a company enters insolvency, a public announcement is made, and creditors get a limited window to file claims. Businesses that miss the announcement often find out months later, when it is too late to be part of the process. Checking takes a minute.

## Three kinds of announcement

IndiaCos tracks **14,943 public announcements** of corporate insolvency and liquidation, newest first. Each is one of three types:

| Type | What it means | What you should do |
|---|---|---|
| **Corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP)** | A creditor (or the company) has started insolvency under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, and an insolvency professional (IP) now runs the company | File your claim with the IP by the deadline in the announcement |
| **Liquidation** | Resolution failed or was not attempted; the company's assets will be sold | File your claim with the liquidator |
| **Voluntary liquidation** | A solvent company is closing itself down | Claim any amounts owed; this is usually orderly |

## What an announcement tells you

Each entry shows the company or LLP name, the type, the announcement date, the **CIN**, the **applicant** who started the process, and the **insolvency professional** in charge. Recent examples show the range of applicants: financial creditors such as SIDBI and finance companies, a homebuyers' association, trustees, operational creditors, and companies applying for their own voluntary liquidation.

## Build it into your process

1. **Before onboarding a vendor or extending credit**, search the company and check its status.
2. **For existing large customers**, check quarterly.
3. **If you find a CIRP**, note the claims deadline immediately and contact the named IP.
4. **Follow the case**: the matter itself is at the NCLT bench, where the orders show the claims, the resolution plan and any liquidation order.

## Combine it with other checks

An insolvency check is one line in a vendor checklist. Others include GST registration status, director details and regulator registrations. Where the announcement carries a CIN, IndiaCos links it to the company page so you can see the rest in one place.
